Data entity relationship model

Three views of one model. Prices are never overwritten: each change creates a dated price version that remembers its source (nomination, change request, AEKO or commodity adjustment). Exchange rates resolve sourcing-level (G IN) first, then project-level (F IN).

Masters setup sequence

Load in this order so each step finds what it depends on. This is also the order of the first prototype sprint.

  1. 1
    User, role, department, head mappingEvery task, reminder and escalation routes through it
  2. 2
    Material group → Buyer, Commodity & milestone set, StakeholdersTask routing and SRM mandatory milestones
  3. 3
    Approval rules, notification templatesNeeded before any CR, commodity price or SRM date is approved
  4. 4
    Project, milestones, exchange ratesSourcing report rows resolve against them
  5. 5
    Supplier, Part (incl. historical pre-fill)Then sourcing report intake becomes an update, not a create
  6. 6
    Car models & volumes, commodity indicesCost-per-car and risk calculations
  7. 7
    Benchmark projectsReference data, kept out of operational KPIs
